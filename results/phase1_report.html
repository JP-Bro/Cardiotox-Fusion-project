1. Dataset Usability Audit

To establish the modeling boundaries for the GNN-Transformer fusion model, we performed a joint audit of the FDA DICTrank labeling set and the LINCS L1000 GSE70138 level-5 expression matrix. Compounds were retained in the usable cohort only if they met the following criteria:

  • Possessed a valid, parseable chemical structure (verified via RDKit).
  • Matched a unique Level-5 perturbation signature (COMPZ) in the HA1E cell line (10.0 µM dose, 24-hour time point).
  • Carried a non-ambiguous DICTrank label mapping to binary classification (No concern = 0; Less/Most concern = 1).

Out of the raw 1,211 labeled DICTrank compounds, a total of 562 compounds met all three requirements, forming our core dataset. The breakdown across the cardiotoxicity label categories is detailed below:

DICTrank Category Model Label Usable Count Percentage of Dataset
Most Concern (High Risk) 1 (Positive) 199 35.4%
Less Concern (Medium Risk) 1 (Positive) 246 43.8%
No Concern (Safe) 0 (Negative) 117 20.8%
Total Usable Set — 562 100.0%

Note on Class Imbalance: The usable cohort has an overall positive (cardiotoxic) rate of 79.2% (445 positive / 117 negative). This substantial class imbalance makes accuracy a misleading performance metric. Consequently, we have designated AUC-PR (Area Under Precision-Recall Curve) as our primary validation metric.

2. Leakage-Free Dataset Splits

To prevent target leakage and ensure realistic validation generalizability, we implemented two separate stratified splitting schemes. In both schemes, splits are frozen at a 70 / 15 / 15 ratio.

A. Drug-Level Splits (Stratified)

Partitioning is done strictly by drug identity rather than individual cell line/dosage signatures. This guarantees that no drug is shared between the train, validation, and test subsets.

Partition Compound Count Positive (Tox) Count Negative (Safe) Count Positive Rate
Train 393 311 82 79.1%
Validation 84 67 17 79.8%
Test 85 67 18 78.8%

B. Bemis-Murcko Scaffold Splits

Compounds are grouped by their Bemis-Murcko carbon scaffolds before partitioning. This represents a more conservative validation strategy, simulating how the model generalizes to entirely unseen chemical families.

Partition Compound Count Positive (Tox) Count Negative (Safe) Count Positive Rate
Train 393 312 81 79.4%
Validation 84 63 21 75.0%
Test 85 70 15 82.4%

3. Verification & Reproducibility

All data steps are fully automated. The random seed is frozen at 42, and the split files are saved in the project repository at:

  • data/splits/drug_split.csv
  • data/splits/scaffold_split.csv

Both split files are frozen and will remain unchanged throughout the baseline and deep modeling phases.
