      approved 2019, LINCS data collected ~2015-2017)

- DESIGN DECISION: salt-suffix stripping (phosphate/hydrochloride/etc.)
  must be applied consistently before LINCS name-matching, same as before
  RDKit graph construction. Even after stripping, structure-based
  (InChIKey) matching is the more robust fallback and should be the
  primary strategy in 03_match_lincs.py, not name-matching alone.

- DOSE/CELL-LINE/TIMEPOINT DECISION (surfaced explicitly, not silent):
    All 6 matched compounds share a common core panel of 7 cell lines
    (A375, HA1E, HELA, HT29, MCF7, PC3, YAPC) x 6 doses x 1 timepoint (24h)
    = 42 signatures minimum per compound.
    CHOSEN DEFAULT: cell_id=HA1E, highest available dose, pert_itime=24h.
    Rationale: HA1E is a relatively non-transformed reference line, used
    consistently across all compounds so far, unlike the 5 cancer lines.

- IMPORTANT CAVEAT FOR WRITE-UP: none of LINCS' core 7 cell lines are
  cardiomyocytes or cardiac-derived. The biology branch will learn a
  general cross-tissue transcriptional toxicity signature, not a
  cardiac-specific one. This matches Seal et al. 2023's own approach and
  reported ~0.76 AUC-ROC ceiling for biology-only -- must be stated
  plainly in results/baseline_comparison.md, not glossed over.
